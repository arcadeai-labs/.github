> Experiments, prototypes, and research from the team
> at [Arcade](https://arcade.dev).

Arcade Labs is where we share early work: things
we're exploring, ideas we're testing, and prototypes
we think are interesting enough to put in front of the
community.

## What lives here

Each repository in this org is an independent
experiment. Some examples of what might land here:

- Research projects exploring new ideas
- Prototypes and proofs of concept
- Demos and reference implementations

## What this is *not*

Arcade Labs is **not** the supported Arcade product.
Specifically:

- Projects here are **not** covered by Arcade's SLAs,
  support agreements, or security commitments
- There is **no** roadmap commitment for any project
  in this org — anything here may move, be rewritten,
  or be archived at any time
- An experiment landing in Arcade Labs is **not** a
  signal that the idea will ship in the Arcade product
- Breaking changes can happen on any commit, without
  notice or migration guides

If you need a supported, production-ready capability,
use [Arcade](https://arcade.dev) and its documented
APIs.

## Project Support

Projects in this organization are provided for your
exploration only, and are not formally supported by
Arcade with Service Level Agreements (SLAs). They are
provided AS-IS, and we make no guarantees of any kind.
Please do not open Arcade support tickets for issues
related to code in these repositories.

Issues discovered while using a project here should be
filed as GitHub Issues on the relevant repo. We'll
review them as time permits, but there are no formal
SLAs.
